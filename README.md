## Recreating "Industry marketing payments to physicians"
**Goal**: recreate the analysis performed in the article [_Industry marketing payments to physicians and
prescription patterns for sacubitril/valsartan in
the USA_](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11874319/pdf/heartjnl-111-4.pdf) by Anju Murayama from the British Medical Journal. 

- Data reconstruction methodolgy can be seen in the [**Open Payments Jupyter notebook**](./notebooks/open-payments.ipynb).
- All analyses performed can be seen in the [**Analysis notebook**](https://github.com/jon-behnken/open-payments/blob/main/notebooks/analysis.ipynb).
