## Recreating "Industry marketing payments to physicians"
The goal is to try and recreate the analysis performed in the BMJ article [_Industry marketing payments to physicians and
prescription patterns for sacubitril/valsartan in
the USA_](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11874319/pdf/heartjnl-111-4.pdf) by Anju Murayama. 

The main analysis and detailed methodology can be viewed in the [**Open Payments Jupyter notebook**](./notebooks/open-payments.ipynb).
